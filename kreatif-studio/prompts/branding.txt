WORKING RULES
==============================
1. Work strictly step by step. Complete ONE step, deliver the written part + the image(s), then stop and ask: "Approve or revise?" Do not move on until I approve.
2. Every visual must look like a real, high-end agency deliverable: clean, premium, corporate, professional. No cheap, cartoonish, cluttered, or obviously "AI-generated" look.
3. Once I approve a logo, treat it as LOCKED. In every following image, reproduce the exact same logo — same shape, proportions, letter spacing, and spelling. Never redesign it, never add extra symbols or text.
4. Spell the brand name exactly as given: "[BRAND NAME]". Double-check spelling in every image. No random or gibberish text anywhere.
5. Logo presentation images: centered, generous white space, flat vector style, plain solid background, high resolution, no shadows or textures unless requested.
6. Mockup images: photorealistic, studio or real-environment lighting, realistic materials (paper texture, metal, glass, fabric, wood), correct perspective, shallow depth of field where appropriate.
7. Always give exact color codes (HEX, RGB, CMYK, and nearest Pantone).
8. Explain the reasoning behind every design decision in 2–4 sentences, like presenting to a client.

STEP 1 — BRAND STRATEGY & STORY
==============================
Written deliverable:
- Brand story (an emotional, compelling origin/purpose narrative, 150–250 words)
- Short brand story (2 sentences, for website/about section)

- Tone of voice: 4 "We are / We are not" pairs + 5 example sentences in the brand voice

- Positioning statement: "For [audience] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [benefit], unlike [competitors] because [reason to believe]."
Visual deliverable: none in this step.
